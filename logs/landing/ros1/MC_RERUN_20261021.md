## Infrastructure changes (no controller change)

- Runner continues after failures, keeps an append-only `ledger.csv`, resumes
  only completed cells with an identical configuration hash, preserves partial
  and retried attempts under `_attempts/`, and retries only failures before the
  landing profile is applied (at most two retries). Post-activation failures
  are final outcomes.
- The target publisher advertises `/target/odom_gt` and `/target/accel_gt`
  when estimate noise is active and the flight recorder keeps them. The
  historical MC2 bags contain only the noisy `/target/odom`; their touchdown
  and violation values were computed against the estimate.
- The planner was rebuilt from current source before commissioning
  (sequential `-j1 -p1`); the previously installed binary predated 5a6ca99.

## Physical scoring

`support/scripts/mc_physical_scoring.py`, identical for historical and new
cells, against the original MC limits recorded in all 360 original
`constraints.csv` files: landing trigger 0.8 m, speed 0.75 m/s, tilt 2.0 deg,
angular rate 0.019 rad/s, glideslope 15 deg with 0.1 m apex offset, LOS 30 deg
below 1.2 m, staging 1.4 m outside 1.1 m, phase-0 limits 3.0 m/s, 11 deg,
0.35 rad/s. Optimizer settings in the preset are tighter and are not used for
scoring.

- `orig_*`: the historical `scenario_metrics.py` window (after the last sample
  above 1.3 m, samples below 0.8 m, trimmed at the force-disarm free fall),
  four landing consequences. Reproduces the historical `samples.csv` values to
  four decimals on checked cells.
- `act_*`: landing activation to touchdown handoff, eight categories with
  trigger logic. Reported separately; not the historical metric.
- Per constraint: maximum positive excess, violation duration at odometry
  resolution (tolerance 1e-4 SI, 0.01 deg), violated flag.
- Truth: `/target/odom_gt` when present. Historical MC2 noisy cells are also
  rescored against the analytic MC2 circle fitted to the estimate (start time
  and constant bias); zero-noise fits are exact and noisy fit residuals equal
  sqrt(2) sigma_p. These rows are labelled `fixed_circle_fit`.

Summaries: `support/scripts/mc_compare_summary.py`. Predicted-horizon results,
when reported, are labelled as predictions.
